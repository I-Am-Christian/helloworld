What can I use for free?+

All six starter lessons, the lesson planner, reading activity, and classroom tools are free to use in this version. You do not need an account.

Can I print a lesson?+

Yes. Open a lesson and select Print / Save PDF. Your browser's print menu lets you print the resource or save it as a PDF.

Does the creator use AI?+

This version uses level-specific templates and the information you enter. The worksheet builder can turn your pasted article, transcript, vocabulary pairs, or grammar examples into a printable activity.

Where are my saved lessons stored?+

Your collection is stored in this browser. It is not shared between devices and may be removed if you clear browser data.
