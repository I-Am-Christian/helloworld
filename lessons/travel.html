The best plan is sometimes no plan

A2 · Travel & culture · 15, 30 or 50 minutes

Ask for directions and make room for a little adventure.

Open teacher and presentation views →

Warm-up

Tell your partner about a place you enjoyed visiting. Was it planned?

Vocabulary

  • turn — change direction
  • opposite — on the other side of
  • nearby — not far away
  • route — the way from one place to another

Read and discuss

Leo wanted to visit a famous museum, but it was closed. He asked a shopkeeper for another idea. She suggested a park nearby. “Turn left at the station. The entrance is opposite the library,” she said. In the park, Leo found a small concert. He stayed for an hour and enjoyed the music.

  1. Why did Leo change his plan?
  2. Where is the park entrance?
  3. What did he find in the park?

Speaking task

A visitor has one free afternoon. Give directions to two places, ask what they enjoy, and choose an activity together.

Useful language: How do I get to…? / Turn left at… / It is opposite…

Reading answer key
  1. The museum was closed.
  2. Opposite the library.
  3. A small concert.
