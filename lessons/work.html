An idea worth speaking up for

B1 · Work & business · 15, 30 or 50 minutes

Make suggestions, ask for clarification, and get heard at work.

Open teacher and presentation views →

Warm-up

When did someone last ask for your opinion? How did you respond?

Vocabulary

  • suggestion — an idea offered for consideration
  • priority — something more important than other things
  • clarify — make something easier to understand
  • feedback — information about how to improve

Read and discuss

At a weekly meeting, Amira suggested a shared checklist for new projects. Her manager worried it would create extra work. Amira explained that the checklist could replace three separate emails. The team agreed to test it on one project and discuss the results next Friday. Amira offered to write the first version.

  1. What did Amira suggest?
  2. Why was the manager unsure?
  3. What did the team agree to do?

Speaking task

Suggest one improvement to how a team works. Your partner raises a concern. Explain the benefit and agree on a small trial.

Useful language: Could we try…? / The main benefit would be… / Could you clarify…?

Reading answer key
  1. A shared checklist for new projects.
  2. The manager thought it would create extra work.
  3. Test it on one project and review it next Friday.
