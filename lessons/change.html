Small changes, new possibilities

B1 · Everyday life · 15, 30 or 50 minutes

Talk about routines, compare experiences, and try something new.

Open teacher and presentation views →

Warm-up

Describe one part of your day that you really enjoy.

Vocabulary

  • routine — things you do regularly
  • challenge — something difficult that tests you
  • confident — feeling able to do something
  • gradually — slowly, over time

Read and discuss

For years, Oliver took the same bus and ate lunch at his desk. Last month, he started walking part of the way to work. At first, he arrived tired. Gradually, the walk felt easier. He also discovered a quiet square where he now eats lunch. He has not changed his whole life, but his days feel different.

  1. What was Oliver’s old routine?
  2. How did walking feel at first?
  3. Where does he now eat lunch?

Speaking task

Choose one small change to a daily routine. Explain how you would try it for a week and how you would decide whether to continue.

Useful language: I used to… / Now I… / At first… but gradually…

Reading answer key
  1. He took the same bus and ate at his desk.
  2. He felt tired.
  3. In a quiet square.
