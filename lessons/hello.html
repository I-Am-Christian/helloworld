Every friendship starts somewhere

A1 · Everyday life · 15, 30 or 50 minutes

Meet someone new. Ask a question. Find something in common.

Open teacher and presentation views →

Warm-up

What do you say when you meet someone new? Practise a greeting with your teacher.

Vocabulary

  • neighbour — someone who lives near you
  • favourite — the thing you like most
  • weekend — Saturday and Sunday
  • together — with another person

Read and discuss

Eva is new in her building. On Saturday, she meets her neighbour, Ben. “Hello! My name is Eva,” she says. Ben likes cooking, and Eva likes food. They go to a café together. Eva asks, “What is your favourite food?” Ben says, “Pizza!”

  1. Who is Ben?
  2. When do they meet?
  3. What food does Ben like most?

Speaking task

Meet a new neighbour. Say your name, ask two questions, and find one thing you both like.

Useful language: My name is… / What is your favourite…? / Do you like…?

Reading answer key
  1. Eva’s neighbour.
  2. On Saturday.
  3. Pizza.
