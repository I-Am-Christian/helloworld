A different view is not the enemy

C1 · Work & business · 15, 30 or 50 minutes

Challenge assumptions while keeping the conversation constructive.

Open teacher and presentation views →

Warm-up

What makes you reconsider an opinion? Give a specific example.

Vocabulary

  • assumption — something accepted without proof
  • concession — accepting part of another person’s argument
  • nuance — a subtle difference in meaning
  • consensus — general agreement within a group

Read and discuss

A team reviewing its working hours initially split into two camps. One favoured complete flexibility; the other wanted everyone available at the same time. Instead of defending fixed positions, they listed the needs behind them. Both valued uninterrupted work and reliable collaboration. They eventually proposed a short shared availability window, leaving the rest flexible. The agreement did not remove every tension, but it made the trade-offs explicit.

  1. What were the two initial positions?
  2. Which needs did both sides share?
  3. Why was the final agreement imperfect?

Speaking task

Debate whether a team should prioritise individual flexibility or collective availability. Restate your partner’s strongest argument before responding.

Useful language: I take your point, although… / That rests on the assumption that… / Could we distinguish between…?

Reading answer key
  1. Complete flexibility versus simultaneous availability.
  2. Uninterrupted work and reliable collaboration.
  3. It made trade-offs explicit but did not remove every tension.
